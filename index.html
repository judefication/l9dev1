Critical Supply Chains Need More Than Transportation

Healthcare and life sciences supply chains operate within complex environments where product requirements, handling conditions, timing, documentation and coordination all matter. When logistics services, information and workflows are disconnected, managing these requirements becomes harder. L9 connects specialised capabilities and workflows to support more coordinated, visible and dependable supply chains.
